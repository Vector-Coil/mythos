Procurement checklist — assets and licenses for Axiom prototype

Fonts
- Cinzel (titles) — check SIL/Google licensing or purchase commercial license if required
- Raleway (body) — Google Font (open)
- Cormorant Garamond (accents) — Google Font (open)

Audio
- Binaural beat stems (ambient loops) — royalty-free or original composition
- UI micro-sounds and transitions — purchase or create custom

Icons & imagery
- SVG motif pack (lines, rings, triangles, shields)
- Background textures (parchment, stone) — ensure commercial use license

Design files
- Source logo (SVG)
- High-res splash and promo images

Third-party services (optional)
- Farcaster integration docs/SDK (free)
- Analytics: Plausible (privacy-friendly, low cost) or Google Analytics
- Email auth provider (if not self-hosting): Supabase/Auth0/Firebase — free tiers available

Notes
- Prefer open-licensed fonts where possible to avoid upfront costs.
- For audio, start with CC0 or royalty-free libraries and replace with bespoke audio if monetizing.
